Bali 🇮🇩

Holiday Inn Resort Bali Nusa Dua

Hotel: Holiday Inn Resort Bali Nusa Dua

A$3,944 total

2 rooms • 10 nights • 3 adults + 3yo + infant • Booking.com 8.7/10 from 1,414 reviews

Beachfront in Nusa Dua/Tanjung Benoa and the strongest live value of the three finalists.

  • Four outdoor pools, including a kids pool with slides and water bucket.
  • Kids club daily; children under 4 need an accompanying adult.
  • Babysitting/nanny can be arranged. Current property Q&A quotes IDR 100,000 net per child/hour — reconfirm.
  • Free baby cot on request; family connecting rooms and kids/family suites exist.
  • Kids Stay & Eat Free benefits may apply under IHG rate conditions.
  • Short transfer after the direct flight — around 20 minutes from DPS in normal conditions.

How it matches us: the 3-year-old gets the pools, playground and beach; the baby has cot/nanny support; and booked nanny time solves the under-4 kids-club restriction when we want a birthday dinner or spa time.

#2 • best childcare + ease

Fiji 🇫🇯

Outrigger Fiji Beach Resort • Coral Coast

Hotel: Outrigger Fiji Beach Resort

A$16,140 total

2 rooms • 10 nights • Booking.com 8.4/10 from 818 reviews

  • Little Riggers club is designed for ages 3–7 — exactly our toddler’s age.
  • Meimei nanny service covers ages 6 months–12 years, available 9am–11pm.
  • Infants 6m–2y use one nanny per child; ages 3–12 use one nanny per two children.
  • Current official Meimei day pricing shows FJD160 / 1 day, FJD310 / 2, FJD450 / 3 and FJD580 / 4; booking rules apply.
  • Family pool with waterslide / Splash Zone, playground and cultural activities.
  • Cribs and connecting rooms available.

How it matches us: this is the cleanest childcare setup found anywhere. Both kids are directly inside the published age ranges, which could give us genuine parent time without improvising.

The issue: at today’s live room rate, Outrigger is over A$12k more than the Bali hotel before packages/promotions. I would only choose it after checking a direct resort package or a second Fiji hotel.

Phuket 🇹🇭

Katathani Phuket Beach Resort • Kata Noi

Hotel: Katathani Phuket Beach Resort

A$6,573 total

2 rooms • 10 nights • Booking.com 8.7/10 from 1,760 reviews

  • Six swimming pools, three kiddie pools, three jacuzzis and a mini waterslide.

Current recommendation

Bali is the sweet spot.

  1. Bali — 9.6/10: best combination of price, October weather, direct flights and family resort infrastructure.
  2. Fiji — 9.4/10: easiest and best childcare, but only makes sense if we beat the current Outrigger room quote.
  3. Phuket — 9.0/10: most exciting resort choice, with weather and return-flight compromises.

My decision rule: if Fiji drops dramatically through a direct package, reconsider it. Otherwise Bali is the rational winner. If we decide the trip should be more about excitement than certainty, Phuket becomes the emotional pick.
